Reviews of Categories  
  
Calculate number of reviews for every business category.  
  
Output the category along with the total number of reviews.  
Order by total reviews in descending order.  
  
DataFrames:  
  
- yelp_business  
business_id:object  
name:object  
neighborhood:object  
address:object  
city:object  
state:object  
postal_code:object  
latitude:float64  
longitude:float64  
stars:float64  
review_count:int64  
is_open:int64  
categories:object  

e.g.
business_id:	G5ERFWvPfHy7IDAUYlWL2A  
name:		All Colors Mobile Bumper Repair  
neighborhood:	''  
address:	7137 N 28th Ave  
city:		Phoenix  
state:		AZ  
postal_code:	85051  
latitude:	33.45  
longitude:	-112.07  
stars:		1.0  
review_count:	4  
is_open:	1  
categories:	Auto Detailing;Automotive
